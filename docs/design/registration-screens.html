AidLedger: registration and duplicate review screens

AH-08 (KAN-42). High-fidelity, mobile-first designs for review by Hadi, in the same style as the pickup screens. Every button is a link: click through the flow and the target screen is highlighted. Sample data is fake.

Covers registration with consent (AH-09, AH-10), the household code and QR card (AH-11), the duplicate warning a volunteer sees (AH-13), and the coordinator's review queue and merge (AH-14, AH-15).

  • Same type scale, colours and buttons as the pickup screens
  • Volunteers never see another family's phone, address or flags
  • Nothing merges without a coordinator
  • No household is saved without consent
  • Every on-screen text has a key, ready for translation

AidLedger: round set-up and pickup screens

MR-08 (KAN-43). High-fidelity, mobile-first designs for review by Hadi. Every button is a link: click through the flow and the target screen is highlighted. Sample data is fake.

Updated after the NGO survey: search is the main way to find a family and QR scan is a shortcut, proxy pickups record who collected, and volunteers always see whether the phone is online and what is still waiting to sync.

  • Body text 17–18 px, titles 30 px
  • Result states: solid colour + icon + word (never colour alone)
  • Contrast ≥ 7:1 on all state colours
  • Main action = one big button at the bottom, thumb reach
  • Works at 360 px wide
  • Search first, scan one tap away
  • Online / offline always visible
